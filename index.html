How it works

Use your echo, but never collide with it.

Your echo repeats the path you took after a short delay. Use it to press plates, open doors, block hazards, and solve each room.

Every move matters. Think ahead, control the timing, and turn your previous movement into the key to the next puzzle.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Echo Gate playable offline?

Yes. Echo Gate supports offline puzzle gameplay.

What makes the game different?

Your previous movement becomes an echo that repeats your path, so your past actions become part of the puzzle.

Which controls are available?

The game includes joystick, arrows, and split-arrow control layouts.
